MTB power

See what every climb took.

Heart rate and estimated power for your mountain-bike rides, from the .fit file your bike computer or watch records.

or drop a .fit file anywhere on the page

Every climb, measured

Climbs are found on their own. Each one gets its length, gain, grade, time and VAM, marked on the chart and listed in a table.

Power without a power meter

Power is estimated from speed, elevation and the weight of rider, bike and gear, with your average on every climb in watts and W/kg.

Stays on your device

Your file is read right here in the browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to make.

How it works

  1. Export the .fit file of a ride from your bike computer, watch or training app.
  2. Open it here, or drop it on the page.
  3. Set your weights and read your climbs: heart rate, power and a chart of the whole ride.
